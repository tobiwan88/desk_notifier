## Order
1. **Click coupon (plate 3).** Press the roof band onto the body band: it should click on with light thumb pressure and lift off by hand without white stress marks; repeat 20 times. Both bands stand on their rear cut face. The body band prints exactly like the real BODY, so the finger flex and force are representative. The roof band is optimistic on the groove surface: here the groove prints as a clean vertical channel, on the real roof it is a notch in a wall tilted 9.2 deg. Judge force and feel, not the groove finish.
2. **Face coupon (plate 4).** Put the real display into the FACE pocket: it should sit snug with no rattle. Push the FACE onto the body band: the 3 arms should click in and hold (lighter than in the house: the coupon has no J2 header, which adds about 10 N of the 15 N total); pull it off again, 20 cycles, no white stress marks. If both pass, the FACE from this plate can be used for the final house.
3. **House (plate 1)** in the main colour and **roof (plate 2)** in the roof colour.
4. **Assemble:** display into J2, board into the BODY cradle, push FACE on (about 15 N, J2 mates in the same stroke), press the roof on. Optional: glue or press a 6.8 g steel plate into the floor recess for extra stability.
5. **Check:** the display does not rattle, the gable above the door does not flex when the roof is pressed, the USB-C plug goes in through the rear funnel, and after 2 hours on Wi-Fi the roof over the ESP32 is only warm.

Report back: click feel and lift-off force, display rattle, gable flex, USB fit, temperature. The model is adjusted from that.
