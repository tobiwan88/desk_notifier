# Printing housing v4 House (A-frame v4d)

One plate with the whole housing (generated by `../make_print_files.py` from `../stl/aframe_b_*`, centred on a 180 x 180 plate, so it fits every Bambu bed):

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `housing_v4d.3mf` | FACE (front down, 10.8 mm), BODY (rear down, 59.1 mm), roof CAP (rim down, tilted 9.2 deg so the rim is flat, 39.6 mm) |
| `housing_v4d_<part>.stl` | the same three objects one by one |

Bambu Studio says "The 3mf file has invalid config, load geometry data only": click OK (the file holds geometry only; positions are kept). Then pick PLA and apply the settings below. Two colours: the three parts are separate objects, so with an AMS give the roof its own filament (right-click the roof > Change filament); without an AMS, print the plate with the roof removed, then the roof alone with the second spool.

Spare FACEs with looser / tighter press-fit ribs (0.10 / 0.30 mm instead of 0.20): `../stl/aframe_b_face_rib010_print.stl`, `../stl/aframe_b_face_rib030_print.stl`.

PLA notes: the snaps are about 40 % stiffer than in PETG (model: FACE arms 0.99 % strain, 3 arms about 21 N to push on; roof fingers 0.45 % strain, about 12 N on and 14 N off for 4 fingers). PLA softens near 55 degC: fine on a desk, not in a sunny window or a hot car.

## Assemble and check
1. Display into the FACE pocket: goes in by hand (0.5 mm per side, 3 ribs touch lightly); J2 holds its height, the pocket only centres it.
2. Slide the bare board into the BODY cradle from the front: no force needed, it rides on the two edge ledges and two floor stilts and does not rock when pressed in the middle, about 0.5 mm play to the rear wall (the last 6 mm are a bit tighter on purpose, to hold the USB end). Plug a USB-C cable in from the rear: it goes in straight and mates fully (the plug's moulded body enters the hole about 2.9 mm).
3. Display onto J2 (black header spacer flat on the socket), push the FACE on: the screen enters the pocket square, glass about 1.9 mm behind the front face, no lean; the ribs need a firm push for the last 6 mm, the 3 arms click, J2 mates in the same stroke and pushes the board back. The FACE must stay when the house is lifted by it. No white marks at the ribs or the arm roots (PLA cracks rather than bends). Too loose or too tight: print a spare FACE.
4. Press the roof on: it clicks on with thumb pressure and lifts off by hand.
5. Optional: glue or press a 6.8 g steel plate into the floor recess for extra stability.
6. After 2 hours on Wi-Fi the roof over the ESP32 is only warm; the gable above the door does not flex when the roof is pressed.

Report back: board slide and USB fit, FACE hold, roof click feel (on / off force), display rattle, temperature. The model is adjusted from that.

## Bambu Studio settings (PLA; PETG in brackets)

Same base as v3 (`../../v3_moon/print/PRINT.md`), with these per-part notes:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Plate | textured PEI |
| Layer height | 0.20 mm |
| Line width | 0.42 mm outer, 0.45 mm inner and first layer |
| Walls | 4 for FACE, BODY and roof (the FACE crush ribs come out solid) |
| Top / bottom shells | 4 layers |
| Infill | 15 % gyroid; 100 % in the click fingers and FACE arms (modifier) |
| Seam | BODY: aligned at the face that is the house floor (in the print frame that is the front, low Y), never on the ridge; roof: at the rim, at a rear corner (DFM_V4 7.1) |

| First layer | 0.2 mm, bed 55 degC [70], 25 mm/s |

| Cooling | fan 100 % after layer 2 [PETG 60 %, 100 % on bridges] |
